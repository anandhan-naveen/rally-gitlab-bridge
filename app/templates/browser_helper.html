Rally browser-session helper

This POC keeps your SSO session inside Rally. It does not read, copy, or store iceSessionId, cookies, or SSO tokens.

POC limitation. The helper only reads Rally data and downloads a JSON snapshot. Nothing is modified in Rally.

1. Download the helper

Download rally-browser-helper.js

2. Run it while logged into Rally

Open Rally normally and sign in with your company SSO. Open your browser Developer Tools → Console, open the downloaded rally-browser-helper.js, copy its contents, paste them into the Rally page console, and press Enter.

The helper will ask whether you want a quarter, feature, or story snapshot and then download a JSON file.

3. Upload the snapshot locally

Return to the bridge dashboard and upload the downloaded JSON. You can then use the normal Preview/Import commands without a Rally API key.
